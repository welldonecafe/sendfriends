The Heartbeat

Why we do what we do, broken down into three simple truths.

The Journey

It starts with a simple "yes". We believe everyone has a role to play in God's global story. Whether you are called to go, pray, or send, the journey is meant to be taken together as friends.

The Unreached

Over 3 billion people have never heard the name of Jesus. They don't have a Bible in their language or a believer in their neighborhood. We are focusing our lives on changing that statistic.

Great Commission

Matthew 28 isn't the "Great Suggestion." It's our primary calling. We exist to make disciples of all nations, prioritizing places where the gospel has not yet taken root.

Current Focus

Target: Japan

Japan is the second largest unreached people group in the world. Despite being highly developed and technologically advanced, less than 1% of the population knows Jesus. We are sending teams to build relationships, plant seeds, and trust God for a revival in the Land of the Rising Sun.

  • 120+ Million Population
  • < 1% Evangelical Christian
  • Deep need for community & hope
