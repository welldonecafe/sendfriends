Jason & Kelsey

To Our Friends & Family,

We first met at a graduation ceremony at Shepherd's Theological Seminary in Cary, North Carolina. Kelsey was finishing up her Masters of Theological Studies while Jason was attending to support his close friend, Noble, who was a fellow classmate of Kelsey's.

An impromptu encounter brought us together, igniting our shared curiosity and zeal to serve Christ on mission. Following a kind and lengthy exchange, we were both unsure whether we would see each other again. However, Jason discovered an unexpected message in Noble's GroupMe later that evening, which started an exhilarating long-distance relationship with multiple trips from Raleigh, North Carolina to Laramie, Wyoming.

Our lengthy talks about faith, travel, music, and food solidified our common interests in experiencing all that God had for us in life and love, which still continues to this day.

Once Kelsey returned home to Calera, Alabama, Jason continued his romantic pursuit, meeting both her family and church community before asking her father, Josh, for her hand in marriage.

We have grown through many adjustments in distance, timing, and backgrounds, but never in heart. Together, we actively minister the Gospel alongside each other, whether in shopping malls, coffee shops, grocery stores, and with the Lord's help, to the ends of the earth.

"This is our story of love, and we're still writing more chapters."

—Jason & Kelsey
